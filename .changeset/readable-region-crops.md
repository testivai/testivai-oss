`get_diff` (and its alias `get_snapshot_diff`) now makes tall full-page captures readable. It still sends the baseline, current and diff images downscaled to fit (a 390×11844 mobile page becomes about 33×987 px, which shows where the changes are but not what they say), and then, when an image had to be downscaled, lists the snapshot's `regions` and crops them at native scale, one image pixel per page pixel, so a reviewer can check text, wrapping and clipping (a page that fits within 1024 px is already sent at native scale and gets no crops, as before). Each crop is a region plus 32 px on every side, made as wide as the page (up to 1280 px) so whole lines show, and labelled with its box and the region's selector; regions whose padded areas meet share a crop while it fits 1280×1024 px, a region inside a box another crop shows joins it, and changes come before shifts. The response stays bounded: up to 3 crops per call, each at most 1280×1024 px and 2,000,000 px in all; an area wider than 1280 px is sampled at 1/2 scale or smaller, and a taller one shows its top rows and gives the `y` to continue from. After a size change each capture is cut to its own bounds, and a crop of the padded band says the shorter capture has no pixels there. New optional inputs fetch one area on demand, without the full-page images: `region` (an index the response lists) or `y` and `height` (a full-width band, which works on a new snapshot too). An image that is not a PNG is now said instead of failing the call. The `triage-visual-diff` rubric, shared with the review action, tells the model to judge text, wrapping and clipping from the crops. Found when the review action triaged testivai/testivai-site#20 and could only judge one page, at thumbnail scale.
